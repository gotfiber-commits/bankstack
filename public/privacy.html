BankJobz

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026

BankJobz ("we") runs a job board for roles at banks and credit unions at bankjobz.com. This policy explains what information we collect, what we do with it, and the choices you have. We keep it in plain language on purpose.

Who we are

BankJobz is a veteran-owned small business. A percentage of every sale goes to help veterans in need. Supporting veterans is funded from our own revenue and doesn't involve your personal information: we don't share your information with any veterans' organization or charity.

What we collect

  • Job seekers. If you create a profile: your name, email, phone, LinkedIn link, location, work preferences, the departments you pick, anything you write in your experience summary, and your résumé. We also extract the text of your résumé so we can rank jobs against your experience. If you apply without a profile, we collect what you enter on the application form.
  • Employers. Your institution's name, your work email, and the content of your job postings. Payments are processed by Stripe; we never see or store your card number.
  • Job alert subscribers. Your email and the departments you choose.
  • Anyone who contacts us. What you send through the Contact us form: your name, email, organization if you give it, topic and message. We use it only to answer you.
  • Everyone. Basic technical logs kept by our hosting provider (such as IP addresses) and small amounts of data in your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and remember preferences. We don't run advertising trackers.
  • Site statistics. To see how the site is used, we count page views on our own servers: the page, the site or campaign link that brought you (such as a search engine, LinkedIn, or a tagged link in one of our emails), your approximate city and state worked out from your IP address, and your device and browser type. We don't store your IP address. To count unique visitors we use a code made from your IP address and browser that changes every day and can't be traced back to you or linked across days. If you create an account, it records the link or campaign that brought you and your approximate city and state, so we know which of our outreach works. Page-view records are deleted after 13 months.

Cookies and tracking

BankJobz doesn't use cookies. We don't use advertising trackers, tracking pixels or third-party analytics, and we don't sell or share your personal information for targeted advertising. Your browser's local storage keeps you signed in; that's needed for the site to work, and signing out clears it. When an employer pays, Stripe's secure checkout page may use its own cookies for fraud prevention, under Stripe's privacy policy. Our pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them.

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, we don't count your visit in our statistics at all.

How we use it

  • To run the board: showing listings, ranking jobs against your profile, and delivering your applications.
  • When you apply to a job, we send your application — including your name, contact details, note and résumé — to that employer. That is the point of applying; the employer then holds its own copy under its own policies.
  • To email you things you asked for: sign-in and confirmation codes, security notices, application confirmations, applicant notifications, and weekly job alerts you subscribed to. Every alert email has a one-click unsubscribe.
  • To bill employers for postings, through Stripe.
  • Keeping employer accounts secure. Passwords are stored only as salted one-way hashes. For each sign-in we keep the browser type and IP address, so you can see where you're signed in and sign out any device, and so we can spot misuse. Employers' private applicant notes and ratings are visible only to that employer.
  • AI writing assistance (only when you use it). If you click an AI assist button — drafting a posting, or tailoring an application — the text involved (your posting notes, or your résumé text and the job description) is sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, to generate the draft, and isn't used to train their models. Drafts are suggestions you review and edit; nothing is posted or sent without you.
  • NMLS ID (optional). If you add your NMLS ID to your profile, we look it up by hand on the public NMLS Consumer Access site and check that it's an individual record matching your name. Employers you apply to see the ID, whether we verified it, and a link to the public record; talent-pool cards show only a "NMLS verified" badge, never the ID. Remove it from your profile any time and the badge goes with it.
  • Talent pool (opt-in only). If you turn on "Show me in the talent pool" on your profile, employers with an active posting can browse an anonymous card: your location, work preference, departments, core-system experience, licenses and your experience summary — never your name, contact details, LinkedIn or résumé. An interested employer can send you an invite, which we deliver by email; your identity is shared only if you choose to apply or reply. You can name employers who must never see your card, and you can leave the pool any time by unticking the box.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share résumés with anyone except the employer whose job you applied to.

Who helps us run the service

Netlify (hosting, file storage, and the approximate location used in our site statistics), Neon (database), Stripe (payments), Resend (email delivery), Google Fonts (the site's fonts), and Anthropic (AI drafting, only when you use an AI assist feature). Each receives only what it needs to do its job.

How long we keep things

Profiles and résumés stay until you ask us to delete them. Applications stay with the posting so employers can review them. Sourced listings expire from the board automatically. Contact form messages are deleted after a year, and site-statistics records after 13 months.

Your choices

  • You can update your profile or replace your résumé any time from your profile page.
  • You can unsubscribe from job alerts with the link in any alert email.
  • To delete your profile, résumé or applications, email privacy@bankjobz.com and we'll remove them within 30 days, except records we must keep (such as billing records).

Children

BankJobz is for working-age adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date above and note the change on the site. Questions: privacy@bankjobz.com.
